## The club

An adult amateur Sunday club affiliated to the London FA, playing across Lambeth, Wandsworth,
Merton and Southwark. England Football Accredited. It has been going long enough to have marked a
25th season.

The June 2026 tour left a trace in Drive: a folder named
`[2026-06-18] Anfiteatro vs Westminster Wanderers`, shared by an Italian club. Whether tour matches
count toward the Pellet Index is open question **Q23**.

The Pellet Index is sized like a single-team competition — regulars record 14 to 24 appearances a
season, not four teams' worth of fixtures — which supports the assumption that it covers the 1st
Team only. Confirmation is open question **Q2**.

### The discrepancy, resolved (Q27 ✅)

The brief said the club had "just played our very first game in the Southern Sunday Football League
Premier Division". The evidence above says they played in the Premier Division in both 2023/24 and
2025/26. Confirmed by the club: this meant *first game of the 2026/27 season*, not a first-ever
Premier Division appearance.

## Practical details for the app

- **Kick-off has no fixed default — confirmed it varies a lot** (10am, 12pm, 10:30, 1pm, or later
  depending on the opponent). Historical fixtures often show 10:30, but don't default or hardcode
  it anywhere; it must be entered per match.
- **Cup codes seen on Full-Time generally:** `FBC`, `MLIP`, `BLUN`, `DTC`, `BHPC`. **Confirmed
  (Q23) the ones Westminster Wanderers actually play:** `MLIP` (~3 games/season), `CC` (~1 game —
  not in the guessed list above), `FBC` (~2 games), all rough estimates. Friendlies and tour games
  don't count toward the Pellet Index. `3G` is a pitch-surface note rather than a competition.
  `PREM` and `CHAM` are the two divisions.
- **A league season is 14 matches**, plus roughly 6 confirmed cup games — a good match for the
  14–24 appearances Pellet regulars actually record. Friendlies don't count; tour games don't
  either, though that rarely matters since the tour happens after the season ends.
- **Walkovers happen.** The 2024/25 results include `H - W` fixtures where a side could not be
  fielded. They must not generate appearances or points.
- **A Drive file named `WWFC | DOBs` exists.** It is personal data and must stay out of this
  repository and out of the product.
